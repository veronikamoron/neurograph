How It Works

1

Document Ingestion

Neuroscience texts are processed and entities (regions, tracts, functions) are extracted.

2

Knowledge Graph Construction

Entities and relationships form a traversable graph alongside a vector index.

3

Multi-Hop Query

Questions trigger both graph traversal and vector search, then an LLM synthesizes the answer.
